1989

PRINTING PRESS

POULTRY CARE LABORATORIES

TEA ESTATES

GRAND PARENT POULTRY

BIO-GAS PLANT

PLASTIC WOVEN BAGS

2016

Consumer products

While staying true to the traditional recipes, Shanwara engineered frozen and chilled foods to maintain freshness, hygienic and quality, sold at every retail outlet.

Mini Grid Project

Bistro & Convenient Store

Inspired by flavors from around the world, Shanwara introduces an intercontinental menu tailored to the traditional tastes and a convenient shop with a franchise program for future expansions.

Joint Venture Mega Grid Project

FIBC Factory
